Mega Man 7 — "Refit"
  A collection of Super NES ROM hacks by DarkSamus993 and JCollins2048

---

[Introduction]
This makes a few modifications to how core mechanics of the game work, as well
as a few minor graphic alterations.

Basically, it makes the game play smoother and more intuitively -- not unlike
Mega Man 5. It makes the game easier, too, I guess. Positive side-effect.

Not all patches work with Puresabe's randomizer, sadly.

[Package Contents]
Note: The Complete patch comes in Headered and Unheadered formats.
      The individual patches are meant for Unheadered ROMs only.

- JDC_Mega Man 7 - Refit Complete
    All-in-one patch.

- JDC_Mega Man 7 - Refit-v050_Faster Charge Shot
    MM5-style charge speed.
    
- JDC_Mega Man 7 - Refit-v050_Player I-Frames Increase 
    MM5-style I-frames.
     
- JDC_Mega Man 7 - Refit-v052_New Damage Data 
    Slightly altered weaknesses for some bosses.
     
- JDC_Mega Man 7 - Refit-v105_No Charge Spark 
    Disables the charge sparkle code. Reduces lag in some instances.
     
- JDC_Mega Man 7 - Refit-v113_Untouchable Rush + Shield Fix 
    Makes Rush invincible, and makes the Protoshield "tink" instead of "bong".

- JDC_Mega Man 7 - Refit-v116a_Stronger Charge Shot
    Ups the Charge Shot strength by 33%. It does 3 damage instead of 2.

- JDC_Mega Man 7 - Refit-v117_Text Fixes
    Little fixes in the script.

- JDC_Mega Man 7 - Refit-v120_Graphic Changes.bps
    Various changes, such as energy capsules, the title cards, the font...

- JDC_Mega Man 7 - Refit-v120_Text Fixes + Password
    Little fixes in the script + "Pass Word" to "Password" on the title.

[Tested CRC32 ROM Hashes]
- 30D835DD -- Mega Man 7 (USA, Headered)
- 2D947536 -- Mega Man 7 (USA, Unheadered)

[Known Bugs]
_v1.0.6 onward_
- Rush still blocks projectiles despite not taking damage

_v1.0.5_
- Protoshield no longer makes any noise, but still blocks shots
  **FIXED IN v1.0.6**

_All versions_
- This ROM hack is ABSOLUTELY NOT COMPATABLE with most other ROM hacks.
  However, individual patches can be added to most US ROM hacks which add the
  benefits of this hack into those.

    A graphic tile editor. Used to edit the graphics.

- Snes9x -- https://github.com/snes9xgit/snes9x

    A ROM patching and patch-making program. Used to make and test patches.
