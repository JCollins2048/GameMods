Super Mario Bros.: Definitive - "Super Mario Bros.: Definitive Tweaks"
  A collection of NES ROM hack addendums by JCollins2048

**IMPORTANT**
This package *requires* a pre-modded SMB Definitive ROM.
You can find the original ROM hack download and information here:
- https://www.romhacking.net/hacks/8509/

---

[Introduction]
As the name suggests, this is a collection of little tweaks and modifications
which may make your Super Mario Bros. Definitive experience even better, or
otherwise "tweak" the gameplay experience.

[Package Contents]
NOTE: There are patches for both MMC3 and VRC3 variants.

- JDC_SMB1Def Tweaks_SMB1 GFX - Replaces all SMB2 graphics with SMB1 ones.
- JDC_SMB1Def Tweaks_SMB1 Luigi Colors - Luigi is white-on-green again.
- JDC_SMB1Def Tweaks_Spiny Egg Fix - Enables the CORRECT Spiny Egg behavior.
  (Bouncing off walls, aiming where the player WILL be.)
- JDC_SMB1Def Tweaks_Warp Scroll Lock Fix - Enables scroll stop at Warp Zone.
 (Unless the player is at the top of the screen, of course.)

[Tested CRC32 ROM Hashes]
- C1A0C9D8 -- Super Mario Bros: Definitive (MMC3 Mapper)
- 6447D139 -- Super Mario Bros: Definitive (VRC3 Mapper)

[Known Bugs]
- Version 1.0
  - When using the "Super Mario Bros. Graphics" patch, the SMB2 Continue screen
    uses a STEEL GIRDER graphic instead of a tiny mushroom.  This is because
    that screen normally uses a moving platform graphic rather than the mushroom
    seen on the title screen.  That's just how it was in SMB2.

[Tools Used]
- XVI32 -- http://www.chmaas.handshake.de/delphi/freeware/xvi32/xvi32.htm
    A simple hex editor. Used to find the title screen tiles.

- YY-CHR -- https://w.atwiki.jp/yychr/
    A graphic tile editor. Used to edit the graphics.

- Famicom Emulator Ultra X -- https://fceux.com/web/home.html
    A Famicom and NES emulator. Used to test the edits.

- Floating IPS -- https://git.disroot.org/Sir_Walrus/Flips
    A ROM patching and patch-making program. Used to make and test patches.
