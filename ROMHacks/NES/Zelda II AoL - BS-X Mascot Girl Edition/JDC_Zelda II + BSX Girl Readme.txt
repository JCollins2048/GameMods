Zelda II: The Adventure of Link - "BS-X Mascot Girl Edition"
  An NES and FDS ROM hack by JCollins2048

**Important**
If you use Floating IPS to apply the FDS patch, you will NEED to make an
EMPTY, FACTORY DEFAULT disk image. Otherwise, it will throw an error.

---

[Introduction]
It's Zelda II!  But, with a completely different - yet, functionally-identical
- character, "BS-X Mascot Girl!" That's the idea, anyway.

Works with the "remove flash" and "restart from palace" hacks.
It also works with the common randomizer (mostly), but I think they've
implemented skins by now.

No, I will not do a "mascot boy" version. Probably.

(You can learn more about BS Zelda and how to play it on a replication cart
emulator at the BS Zelda HomePage: https://bszelda.zeldalegends.net/)

[Package Contents]
NOTE: There are patches for both NES and FDS variants.

- JDC_Zelda II + BSX Girl -- The main hack.
- JDC_Zelda II + BSX Girl (Just Link) -- Replaces just Link's graphics.

[Tested CRC32 ROM Hashes]
- C1A0C9D8 -- Zelda II - The Adventure of Link (USA)
- C00ED08F -- Link no Bouken - The Legend of Zelda 2 (Japan)

[Known Bugs]
_Version 1.0_
- When using this on a randomized ROM, it corrupts some of the graphics.
  This is because the randomizer relocates some graphics.
  No, I will not make a "randomizer" version.

    A Famicom and NES emulator. Used to test the edits.
